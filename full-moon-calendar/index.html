Questions

About full moons

When is the next full moon?

Full moons come about every 29.5 days. The box at the top of this page counts down to the next one in your own time zone, and the tables list every full moon from 2026 to 2028.

What is a supermoon?

A supermoon is a full moon that happens while the Moon is near the closest point of its orbit, so it looks slightly bigger and brighter. On this calendar, full moons closer than 361,500 km are marked as supermoons and those farther than 405,000 km as micromoons.

What is a blue moon?

A blue moon is the second full moon in one calendar month. It happens about every two to three years. The Moon does not turn blue; the name only refers to the rare timing.

Why do full moons have names?

Traditional names such as Wolf Moon, Harvest Moon and Hunter's Moon come from North American and European folk calendars and describe the season. The Harvest Moon is the full moon closest to the September equinox, and the Hunter's Moon is the one after it.

Is the full moon on the same date everywhere?

The full moon happens at one instant for the whole world, but the local date can differ by a day depending on your time zone. Every city page on Moonwatch lists the full moons in that city's local time, with moonrise.
